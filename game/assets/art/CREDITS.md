# Placeholder art credits

The PNGs in this folder are placeholders taken from the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles
(https://github.com/crawl/tiles, curated releases `Nov-2015` and `Ancient/Oct-5-2010`), which their
artists released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Tiles that
crawl/tiles lists as having unclear licensing were not used. Player sprites are composed from that
set's paper-doll layers; a few icons are DCSS sprites placed on the DCSS spell-icon frame, and the
Gate banners are compositions of DCSS tiles. `manifest.json` records the source file(s) of every
placeholder.

Where the DCSS set had nothing suitable (white UI glyphs, some status icons, the slash, heal and
level-up effects, the 9-patch UI skin, the title logo), the art was drawn for this game by
`tools/art/pixel_art.py` and is released under CC0 1.0 too (manifest source "original").

Fonts in `fonts/` are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (license text next to each file):
Jersey 10 by Sarah Cadigan-Fried (The Soft Type Project) and Pixelify Sans by Stefie Justprince,
both from Google Fonts. The title logo's lettering is set in Jersey 10.

No attribution is required under CC0; this note is kept as a courtesy and for provenance.
Thanks to the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup artists.
