# Placeholder art credits

The PNGs in this folder are placeholders taken from the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup tiles
(https://github.com/crawl/tiles, curated releases `Nov-2015` and `Ancient/Oct-5-2010`), which their
artists released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Tiles that
crawl/tiles lists as having unclear licensing were not used. Player sprites are composed from that
set's paper-doll layers. `manifest.json` records the source file(s) of every placeholder.

No attribution is required under CC0; this note is kept as a courtesy and for provenance.
Thanks to the Dungeon Crawl Stone Soup artists.
